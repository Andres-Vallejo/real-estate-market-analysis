# Executive Summary

## Recommendation

Chico and Usaquen show premium pricing, while Centro and selected Chapinero units provide stronger affordability per square meter with high transit access. Sellers with slow listings and large positive pricing gaps should consider price adjustments or repositioning.

## Key Insights

- Price per square meter is the cleanest benchmark for comparing property value across neighborhoods.
- Days on market separates attractive inventory from listings that may be mispriced.
- Transit score and condition score help explain why similar-size properties can price differently.
- A model-based pricing gap creates a practical watchlist for overpriced or undervalued listings.

## Portfolio Narrative

This project demonstrates how a data analyst can combine market KPIs, feature engineering, SQL, and a lightweight predictive model to support real estate decisions.
